How it works

Course context in the tools students already use

Kelvin is a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. An AI assistant connects to it to look up course information while helping a student.

01

Connect

Add Kelvin as a custom MCP connector in an AI assistant that supports remote MCP.

02

Ask

Kelvin supplies the relevant course topics, misconception cards, and tutoring approaches.

03

Review

Students can see their saved learning reflections. Instructors can explore course content and class-level trends in the dashboard.
